Privacy Policy

MyCV.i.ng

Last Updated: 16th June, 2025

At MyCV.i.ng, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our service to create your CV website.

What Information We Collect

When you use our service, we may collect the following information:

Your full name

Email address

Phone number (WhatsApp preferred)

Your CV (PDF or Word format)

Payment receipts or transaction details

Optional sample links or additional design preferences

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as passwords, bank details, or biometric data.

How We Use Your Information

We use your submitted information solely for the purpose of:

Creating and publishing your CV website

Contacting you for updates or delivery status

Providing support or customer service

Improving our service based on feedback

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third-party services or marketers.

Document Confidentiality

Your CV and all submitted files are treated as confidential. They are only accessed by our team to build your website and are never published, shared, or stored publicly without your consent.

We delete raw CV files after your site is successfully delivered, unless you request long-term update support.

Payment Security

Payments are handled via a secure third-party provider (e.g., Paystack or Flutterwave). MyCV.i.ng does not store or process your credit/debit card details. All payment transactions are encrypted and handled securely through the payment platform.

Cookies and Tracking

We may use basic website tracking tools like cookies to improve site performance and understand user behavior. These tools do not collect personally identifiable information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

Request deletion of your personal data

Ask for corrections or updates to your details

Withdraw consent at any time

To do any of the above, please contact us directly.

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service and for basic support. If you wish to have your data deleted after delivery, just let us know.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. All changes will be reflected on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.
