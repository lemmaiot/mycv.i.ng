# MyCV.i.ng 🚀

**Turn your CV into a personal website — in just 24 hours, for only ₦2,500.**

MyCV.i.ng is a simple, fast, and affordable platform that transforms traditional CVs into sleek personal websites hosted under a branded domain (e.g. `www.mycv.i.ng/yourname`). It’s designed to help professionals, freelancers, and job seekers stand out online with zero tech effort required.

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ One-time fee of ₦2,500 — no subscriptions
- ✅ Fully hosted personal CV website
- ✅ Mobile-friendly and recruiter-optimized
- ✅ Live in 24 hours or less
- ✅ No design or technical skills needed
- ✅ Optional style customization with sample link

---

## 📦 How It Works

1. **User uploads** their CV (PDF/Word) and optional sample reference
2. **Payment** of ₦2,500 is made via a secure link
3. **Submission** includes CV + payment receipt via form or WhatsApp
4. **Website is created** and goes live at `www.mycv.i.ng/username` within 24hrs
